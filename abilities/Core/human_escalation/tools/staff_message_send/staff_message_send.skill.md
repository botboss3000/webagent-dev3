# staff_message_send

## Usage

- Leave a message for staff to follow up: the visitor's contact details and request.
- Ask for consent first and include at least an email or phone number.
- Never collect payment card or government ID numbers.

Confirm the visitor agrees to be contacted, then save: name, email and/or phone (at least one), kind (question, booking, callback, feedback, other) and a one-paragraph message with the details staff need (dates, party size, service). Repeating the same call is safe. Tell the visitor staff will follow up; do not promise a time.
