# request_human

## Usage

- Ask this agent's staff to take over the conversation, when the user asks for a person or needs something you cannot do.
- Staff are notified in the app; tell the user someone will join.

Notifies staff that this conversation needs a person. It is refused when the agent's help policy is off; then offer `staff_message_send` instead. Staff may join and pause you; while paused, visitor messages go to staff only.
