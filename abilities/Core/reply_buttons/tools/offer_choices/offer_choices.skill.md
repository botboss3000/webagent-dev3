# offer_choices

## Usage

- Show the visitor up to 8 reply buttons (for example time slots or menu sections).
- A click sends that label as the visitor's own next message; it authorizes nothing by itself.

Give 1–8 short single-line labels. The visitor sees buttons under your message; clicking one sends its label as their next message. Do not use it for yes/no confirmations of payments or anything that needs staff approval.
