Main Notes are this agent's notes across all its sessions: facts, decisions, open work and guidance for its other sessions. A note can be guidance to follow, such as how to respond.

The whole of Main Notes is above; changes made by other sessions appear later in the conversation as updates.

Add or update a note after a meaningful decision, milestone, blocker, handoff or completion, not after every step. Update a matching topic or entry instead of adding a near-duplicate; read it with `main_notes_read` first to get its current IDs and revision. Mark finished items done or cancelled. Record only the timing or conditions the user actually asked for. A saved note never authorizes an action by itself. Main Chat schedules note-owned follow-ups; don't schedule them yourself.
