WebAgent Browser Extension

This Chrome extension lets your WebAgent agent work in its own browser window on the sites your organization allows. You see its pointer and typing, and you can take over at any moment by clicking or typing yourself.

  2. Unzip webagent-browser-extension.zip into a folder you will keep.
  3. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Go back to your WebAgent chat and click anywhere on the page. The extension links to your signed-in account by itself.

Desktop Chrome, Edge or Brave. It needs permission to control its own tabs (Chrome shows “WebAgent Browser Extension started debugging this browser” while the agent works). To stop it, unlink from the extension’s toolbar button or remove the extension.
